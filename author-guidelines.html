FOR AUTHORS

Author Guidelines

Preparation requirements for manuscripts submitted to RLLS.

Submission status: The final submission system is not yet activated. These guidelines are a working framework and should be formally approved before launch.

1. Article types

The journal may consider original research articles, review articles and selected scholarly contributions within its scope. The final article categories and word limits will be specified in the approved author instructions.

2. Required manuscript components

  • Title of the manuscript
  • Author name(s), affiliation(s) and contact information
  • Abstract and keywords
  • Main text with appropriate sectioning
  • References
  • Tables and figures where applicable
  • Funding, conflict-of-interest and ethics statements where applicable

3. Technical preparation

ItemWorking requirement
FileMicrosoft Word (.docx)
LanguageEnglish, Persian or Russian, subject to the journal's final policy
AbstractConcise, self-contained summary of purpose, method, findings and conclusion
ReferencesComplete and consistent bibliographic information; DOI should be supplied when available
OriginalityNot previously published and not simultaneously submitted elsewhere

4. Review process

Submissions are intended to undergo initial editorial screening for scope, completeness and basic scholarly quality. Suitable manuscripts may then be assigned to external reviewers. The exact review model and decision stages will be published before the journal begins receiving manuscripts.

5. Before submission

  • Check that all authors have approved the manuscript.
  • Check references, quotations, tables and figures for accuracy.
  • Remove identifying information if anonymous review is required.
  • Declare conflicts of interest and funding where applicable.
  • Ensure that permissions have been obtained for copyrighted third-party material.
