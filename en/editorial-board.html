Editorial structure

Editorial Board

Scientific and transparent introduction of those responsible for the journal’s policy, evaluation and content direction.

The editorial board is a core element of a journal’s scholarly credibility. Its members should have relevant expertise and experience aligned with the journal’s declared scope. This page provides an independent profile for each member so that academic information and professional links can be reviewed in a clear and structured way.

Names and roles are listed only after formal acceptance of responsibility. Unconfirmed names or roles are not published.

Information to be displayed for each member

  • Official high-quality photograph.
  • Full name and academic title.
  • Role on the editorial board or specialist responsibility.
  • University, institute or organisation and country.
  • Areas of expertise and research keywords.
  • Short scholarly introduction and selected experience where relevant.
  • ORCID and institutional or research profile, if provided.
  • Professional links (Google Scholar, ResearchGate, LinkedIn or personal website) only when confirmed and supplied by the member.

Transparency principles

Information on this page must be consistent with the members’ official and public records. Affiliation, role, academic title and professional links are published after information is received from the person or a verifiable official source, and will be updated if they change.

This page is designed to present the journal’s scholarly capacity accurately and does not replace the peer-review policy. The role of each member in decision-making, review or advisory processes must be stated in accordance with the journal’s official policies.
