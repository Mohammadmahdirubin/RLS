Privacy

General information about information submitted through the website.

Personal information

Information submitted through website forms is intended for the purposes stated on the relevant form, such as processing a manuscript submission or responding to correspondence.

Manuscripts

Authors should avoid including unnecessary personal or confidential information in uploaded files. Submission data may be processed by the third-party form service used by the website.

The journal will use submitted information only as necessary for editorial and administrative purposes and will not publish private author information without an appropriate basis.

Updates

This privacy notice will be expanded and formally approved before regular publication operations begin.
