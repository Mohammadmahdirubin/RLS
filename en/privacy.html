Data protection

Privacy Notice

How information is collected, used and protected on the journal website and in editorial processes.

Russian Language Studies respects the privacy of authors, reviewers, readers and other users. This notice outlines the general framework for handling information related to the website and editorial activities.

Information that may be collected

  • Contact and identity details: name, affiliation, email and other details you provide when corresponding or submitting a manuscript.
  • Manuscript-related materials: article text, supplementary files and data submitted with the manuscript.
  • Technical website data: standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and access time) needed for security and site performance.

Purposes of use

  • Managing submission, peer review, editing and publication
  • Editorial communication with authors and reviewers
  • Improving security, accessibility and quality of the website
  • Publishing necessary scholarly information about accepted articles (such as author names and affiliations)

Confidentiality of peer review

Under double-blind peer review, the identities of authors and reviewers are kept confidential. Review correspondence, reviewer reports and confidential editorial materials are shared only with authorised persons (the editorial team and, where needed, the relevant reviewers) and are not disclosed for purposes unrelated to scholarly evaluation.

Sharing of information

Personal information is not shared with third parties without a legitimate basis or consent, except where necessary for publication, peer review, legal requirements, or limited technical services (such as institutional email) required to operate the journal.

Retention and security

The journal seeks to protect information with reasonable technical and organisational measures and to retain it only for as long as needed for editorial purposes and archival requirements.

Your rights

You may request correction or updating of contact details held in correspondence with the journal. For privacy-related questions, contact the editorial office at mm.rubin@ut.ac.ir.

Updates to this notice

This page will be updated when a full manuscript-management system is launched or when procedures change. The current version covers the general policy of the website and editorial processes.
