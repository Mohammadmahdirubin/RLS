Official manuscript submission system
Manuscript submission, file uploads, author information and review tracking are handled through the journal’s Open Journal Systems (OJS) platform.
Before submitting, please read the Author Guidelines and prepare your manuscript accordingly. The main manuscript file must be anonymized for peer review.

Submit through OJS

To submit a new manuscript, sign in to your account. If you do not have an account yet, register with the journal and select the Author role during registration.
