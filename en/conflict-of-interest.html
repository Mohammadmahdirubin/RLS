Conflict of Interest

Principles for disclosure and management of conflicts of interest.

Definition

A conflict of interest exists when a personal, financial, professional, or institutional relationship could influence, or reasonably appear to influence, scientific judgment or editorial decision-making.

Authors

Authors must disclose relevant conflicts arising from the research, funding, employment, or institutional relationships connected with the manuscript.

Reviewers

Reviewers must disclose any relationship or interest that could compromise the independence of their assessment before accepting a review.

Editorial team

Editorial personnel with a conflict of interest must withdraw from decisions concerning the affected manuscript and the matter should be assigned to an appropriate alternative decision-maker.

Transparency

Operational details of conflict-of-interest declarations will be finalized alongside the formal submission and peer-review procedures.
