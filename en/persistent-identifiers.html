Article Identifiers

Persistent Article Identifiers

The journal’s policy on DOI and other persistent identifiers.

Current Status

The journal does not currently assign DOIs to its articles. Until a persistent identifier service is formally established, each published article has a dedicated and stable web address on the journal website.

DOI and Future Identifiers

If the journal formally adopts DOIs or another persistent identifier system such as Handle or ARK, the registered identifier will be added to the article page and article metadata after official registration. No DOI or identifier will be assigned before formal registration.

Link Persistence

Each article’s dedicated URL should resolve to the same published article on the journal website. If the website structure changes, the journal will seek to preserve published article links or redirect them to their new official locations.

ORCID

The journal supports the inclusion of authors’ ORCID iDs in article information and related metadata.
