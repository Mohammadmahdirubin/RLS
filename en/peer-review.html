Peer Review

Peer Review Process

A transparent framework for the scholarly assessment of submitted manuscripts.

Implementation status: This page defines the journal's policy framework. The final review model and reviewer arrangements will be confirmed before formal publication begins.

Review stages

1. Initial screening

Manuscripts are checked for scope, apparent originality, completeness, structure and compliance with author requirements.

2. Specialist assessment

Eligible manuscripts will be sent to independent reviewers with relevant subject expertise.

3. Editorial assessment

Reviewer reports are considered together with the manuscript's scholarly quality before an editorial decision is made.

4. Revision and reassessment

When revision is requested, the corresponding author should provide a revised manuscript and a documented response to reviewer comments.

Evaluation criteria

  • Fit with the journal's aims and scope
  • Originality and significance of the research problem
  • Strength of the theoretical framework and methodology
  • Validity of analysis and evidence
  • Coherence and quality of scholarly writing
  • Accuracy and appropriateness of references
  • Scientific contribution and relevance

Reviewer responsibilities

Reviewers should evaluate manuscripts objectively, confidentially, constructively and within a reasonable timeframe. They should disclose relevant conflicts of interest and must not use confidential manuscript information for personal purposes.

Confidentiality

Manuscripts and review reports are treated as confidential during the review process. Reviewer identities will not be disclosed except where required by the journal's formal policy or applicable law.

Editorial decision

Decisions on acceptance, revision or rejection are made within the journal's editorial framework and on the basis of scholarly assessment. A request for revision does not guarantee acceptance.
