Author Guidelines

Requirements for preparing and submitting manuscripts to Russian Language Studies.

Downloadable files:

In order to publish an article in the journal Russian Language Studies, the following requirements must be observed.

A. Academic Requirements

  1. The article must be the result of scientific research conducted by the author or authors. If it is established at any stage that the article has been extracted from other sources or articles without proper citation, the manuscript will be removed from the review or acceptance process, and legal action will be taken.
  2. Submitted articles must meet the standards of scholarly journals and must be one of the following types: Original Research Article, Review Article, or Book Review.

B. Manuscript Preparation and Submission of the Main File

The main manuscript file must not contain any identifying information about the authors, including names, postal addresses, email addresses, telephone numbers, or similar details. Do not use an author's name in the manuscript filename. Author information must be entered in a separate file entitled “Author Details File”. This file should include the full name, institutional affiliation (university), email address, telephone number, and other relevant information. If there is more than one author, the corresponding author must also be clearly identified.

  1. The article must follow the structure of a scholarly article and observe the principles of academic writing.
  2. Submitted manuscripts should be formatted as follows:
    1. 2.1. The article title must appear on the first page, centered, in Nazanin B, 14 pt.
    2. 2.2. The Persian abstract must contain 150–250 words and be written in Nazanin B, 12 pt. Persian keywords should be listed at the end of the Persian abstract, from general to specific, with 4–6 keywords.
    3. 2.3. The main text must contain 4,000–8,000 words and be written in Nazanin B, 13 pt.
    4. 2.4. Line spacing must be single. Except for the first paragraph of each subsection, all other paragraphs must begin with an indentation of five spaces.
    5. 2.5. In-text citations must be formatted as follows:
      References to books or articles should appear in parentheses in the order of author surname, year, and page number. Example: (Rezaei, 1402: 67). Where necessary, the title of the work may be used instead of the author's name.
    6. 2.6. The English equivalents of proper names, Latin terms, and foreign expressions must be given in a footnote once, at their first occurrence in the article.
    7. 2.7. The conclusion must be complete and comprehensive and appear at the end of the article.
    8. 2.8. The reference list must be arranged alphabetically and presented at the end of the article according to the following formats:
      • Book: Author surname, first name (year of publication). Book title, editor or translator (year of edition). Place of publication: Publisher.
      • Journal article: Author surname, first name (year of publication). “Article title.” Journal title, volume, issue, page number(s) on which the article is published.
    9. 2.9. The English abstract must be written in Times New Roman, 11 pt. English keywords must be translations of the Persian keywords and must be listed at the end of the English abstract.

3. Page and File Formatting

  1. 3.1. Articles must be submitted to the journal's submission system as a Word file using the journal's format.
  2. 3.2. If the article contains poetry, the poems must be typed and formatted inside a table.
  3. 3.3. If the article contains a chart or graph created in Excel, the corresponding Excel file must also be submitted with the manuscript. Care must be taken with the size of tables and figures. Tables and figures must not exceed the defined page dimensions.
  4. 3.4. Words must be typed separately, with appropriate spacing and half-space conventions observed. Examples include: آن‌ها، کرده‌اند، مهم‌ترین، می‌آید.
  5. 3.5. Punctuation marks such as periods, commas, semicolons, colons, and similar marks must be attached to the preceding word.
  6. 3.6. The orthography used throughout the article must comply with the Persian Orthography Guide published by the Academy of Persian Language and Literature.

C. Article Evaluation Process

Initially, manuscripts are registered through the article-submission section of the journal's system. They are then presented by the executive manager at an editorial board meeting. Following the editor-in-chief's consideration and evaluation by the editorial board, manuscripts that meet the necessary requirements are sent to two or more reviewers. After the reviewers' comments are received, the review outcome is discussed at an editorial board meeting. If revisions are required, the reviewers' comments will be sent to the author. Finally, if the manuscript receives sufficient scores, an acceptance letter will be sent to the author. The minimum time for the initial evaluation of manuscripts is one to two weeks. If a manuscript is sent to the main reviewers, the peer-review process will take at least one month.

D. Initial Acceptance Requirements

  1. The article must be formatted according to the journal's style guidelines.
    1. 1.1. The article must include, in order: title, abstract, keywords (4–6), introduction (including the research problem and objective), literature review, research methodology, theoretical foundations, data analysis, conclusion, and references (APA).
  2. Articles derived from theses must be accompanied by the supervisor's approval, and the supervisor's name must be entered and stated in the submission system. The order of authors' names and the corresponding author must be determined at the time of submission. After submission, the order of authors cannot be changed, and no author can be added or removed. Authors must carefully enter their institutional affiliations; these also cannot be changed. Responsibility for failure to observe these requirements rests with the authors.
  3. The author must undertake that the article has not been submitted simultaneously to another journal and will not be submitted elsewhere until its rejection by Russian Language Studies has been established. The commitment form available on the website must be completed, signed, and submitted with the article.
  4. The article must not have been previously presented or published in any journal or conference.
  5. Submitted articles will under no circumstances be returned.
  6. The author or authors are responsible for the content of each article in all respects.
  7. For articles that use phonetic transcription, submission of the relevant font file and the article PDF is mandatory.

Required Forms

To submit an article, the following forms must be completed and uploaded with the specified titles:

  • Conflict of Interest Form: complete the form and upload it in the file-upload section under the title “Conflict of Interest Form.”
  • Commitment Form: complete the commitment form and upload it in the file-upload section under the title “Letter to the Editor.”

Only the corresponding author has access to view, edit, and submit information after the article has been submitted and during the review process. Therefore, when submitting the article, make sure that the corresponding author will be able to access the system.

To follow the status of your manuscript, please use the journal's submission system and, as far as possible, avoid telephone contact. Emails may sometimes encounter errors or disruptions. Authors are therefore requested to log into the journal system periodically and check the latest status of their manuscript in their profile.
