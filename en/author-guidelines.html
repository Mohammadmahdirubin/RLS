The journal accepts manuscripts in Persian, English and Russian. Authors should download the template that matches the language of their article.

The author guidelines cover the journal’s subject scope, originality and submission conditions, required files, suggested article structure, citation style (APA 7th edition), research ethics and a pre-submission checklist.

The main manuscript file must contain no author identifiers so that anonymous review is preserved. Full author details are provided in a separate form.

After reading the guidelines and completing the files you may proceed to Submit a manuscript.
