Author Guidelines

Initial requirements for manuscript preparation and submission.

Current status: These guidelines are an establishment-phase framework and will be finalized before formal publication begins.

Manuscript types

  • Research Articles
  • Review Articles
  • Short Communications
  • Book Reviews and other scholarly contributions

Submission requirements

Manuscripts should be original, clearly structured and relevant to the journal scope. Authors should provide a title, abstract, keywords, author affiliations and contact information, and ORCID identifiers where available.

Research integrity

Authors must disclose funding, conflicts of interest and relevant research-ethics information. Submitted work should not be under consideration by another journal.

Files and formatting

The online submission form accepts manuscript files in DOC, DOCX or PDF format. Detailed formatting, reference style and anonymization requirements will be specified in the final version of the guidelines.

Peer review

Eligible manuscripts will first undergo editorial screening and may then be sent for external review according to the journal's formal policy.
