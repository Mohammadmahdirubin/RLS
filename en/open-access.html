Open Access Policy

Russian Language Studies is an open access journal. The full text of published articles is available immediately and free of charge to all users without registration, subscription, or pay-per-view access.

Published articles are distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license, permitting reuse, redistribution, translation, and adaptation subject to the license terms and appropriate attribution.

Copyright and License

Authors retain their rights under the journal's copyright policy, while the CC BY 4.0 license defines the reuse rights granted to readers and other users.

Author Fees

Publication in Russian Language Studies is completely free of charge. The journal charges no fees for manuscript submission, peer review, editorial processing, acceptance, article processing charges (APCs), typesetting, color publication, language editing, or publication. No other mandatory publication fee is charged to authors.
