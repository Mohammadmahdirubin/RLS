Post-Publication Policy

Corrections and Retractions

Procedure for dealing with errors and serious issues after publication.

Corrections

If a correctable error is identified after publication, the journal may publish a correction notice and keep a clear link between the notice and the original version of the article.

Retractions

In cases such as serious research misconduct, fundamental error or violation of publication principles, an article may be retracted. The decision must be documented and transparent.

Expression of Concern

Where a serious concern about an article exists but the investigation has not yet reached a final conclusion, an expression of concern may be published if necessary.

Record transparency

Records of corrections or retractions must be presented so that the reader can identify the status of the article and the reason for the editorial action.
