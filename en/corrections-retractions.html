Post-Publication Policy

Corrections and Retractions

Procedures for handling errors and serious concerns after publication.

Corrections

When a correctable error is identified after publication, the journal may publish a correction notice and maintain a clear link between the notice and the original article.

Retractions

In cases such as serious research misconduct, fundamental error, or violation of publication principles, an article may be retracted. The decision should be documented and transparent.

Expression of Concern

When a substantial concern exists but an investigation has not reached a final conclusion, an expression of concern may be issued where appropriate.

Record transparency

Correction and retraction records should allow readers to identify the current status of an article and the reason for the editorial action.
