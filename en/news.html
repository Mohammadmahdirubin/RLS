29 October 2026

Autumn 2026 Call for Papers

Russian Language Studies invites scholars, researchers and students to submit original research articles and review papers for its Autumn 2026 issue. Submissions are welcome in Russian linguistics, Russian-language teaching, Russian literature, culture and translation. Manuscripts are accepted in Persian, Russian and English. Submission deadline: 1 December 2026 (10 Azar 1405). Publication date: 21 December 2026 (30 Azar 1405). All submissions will undergo an initial screening and scholarly peer review; publication is subject to final acceptance. Please visit the journal website for submission requirements and author guidelines.

27 October 2026

International cooperation in scholarly peer review

Russian Language Studies has expanded its international academic cooperation through agreements with two scholarly journals in Spain and Poland. Cooperation with the University of Granada, Spain, was established under an agreement with Professor Rafael Guzmán Tirado, proprietor of the Spanish Yearbook of Russian Studies, Cuadernos de Rusística Española (CRE). Cooperation with the University of Gdańsk, Poland, was established under an agreement with Dr. Michał Daszkiewicz, a professor at the University of Gdańsk’s Faculty of Social Sciences and editor-in-chief of Educational Role of Language (ERL). Within this framework, Dr. Mahnaz Norouzi, Associate Professor of Russian, has been nominated for peer-review cooperation with CRE, while Dr. Mehdi Mohajer, Assistant Professor of French, and Dr. Shima Ebrahimi, Associate Professor of Linguistics, have been nominated to cooperate with ERL. These agreements aim to facilitate the exchange of peer-review expertise, strengthen scholarly evaluation and build closer links between academic journals.

26 October 2026

First journal version submitted for ISSN registration

The first version of Russian Language Studies was submitted to the National Library of Iran and the Ministry of Culture and Islamic Guidance for review as part of the process of obtaining an International Standard Serial Number (ISSN). The registration process is being followed up with the relevant authorities; the final outcome will be announced once officially confirmed.

25 October 2026

First issue completed with seven research articles

The first issue of Russian Language Studies has been completed and made available on the journal’s official website, featuring seven research articles. This inaugural collection marks an important step in the journal’s scholarly activity and brings together research related to the Russian language, language education, linguistics, literature and culture.

The publication of the seventh article on 25 October 2026 completed the issue. Full texts and bibliographic information for all seven articles are available in the Published Articles section. The cover of the first issue appears alongside this announcement; selecting it opens the complete issue in PDF format.

17 September 2026

Journal launch and publication of the first article

Russian Language Studies began its online activity with the publication of its first article on 17 September 2026. The journal provides an academic platform for research on the Russian language, language education, linguistics, literature, culture and translation, and accepts scholarly manuscripts in Persian, Russian and English.
