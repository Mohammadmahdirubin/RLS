9 November 2026

An Important Step Toward DOI Processing and Assignment at Russian Language Studies

As part of its efforts to strengthen scholarly publishing standards, improve article discoverability and facilitate the citation of research, the Russian Language Studies journal has established the technical infrastructure required to process and assign Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) through the mEDRA system.

With article metadata processing now available through this system, the journal is moving forward toward registering and assigning DOIs to its published articles. This is an important step toward enhancing the journal’s international visibility, scholarly accessibility and the citability of its publications.

Notice on DOI Processing and Assignment Fees

The journal does not charge any fees for manuscript submission, peer review or editing.

A fee of 750,000 Iranian tomans per article will be charged solely for articles that receive final acceptance after the peer-review process. This fee covers DOI processing, acquisition and assignment.

The fee is payable only after final acceptance and has no influence on peer review, scholarly evaluation or the editorial decision. Authors are not required to pay any amount at submission or during the review process.

Full details are available on the journal’s Publication Fees page.

2 November 2026

Updates to the scholarly and editorial requirements in the Author Guidelines

The Author Guidelines of Russian Language Studies have been revised to clarify manuscript length, reference requirements and the use of up-to-date sources. Research articles should contain 3,000–7,000 words, while review articles and exceptional cases may extend to 9,000 words. Research articles should cite 15–40 sources; review articles may cite up to 60. For contemporary topics, at least half of the references should have been published within the past ten years, while established older sources remain appropriate for classical topics. Source quality, credibility and relevance take precedence over quantity. Authors should consult the updated Author Guidelines before submission.

27 October 2026

International cooperation in scholarly peer review

Russian Language Studies has expanded its international academic cooperation through agreements with two scholarly journals in Spain and Poland. Cooperation with the University of Granada, Spain, was established under an agreement with Professor Rafael Guzmán Tirado, proprietor of the Spanish Yearbook of Russian Studies, Cuadernos de Rusística Española (CRE). Cooperation with the University of Gdańsk, Poland, was established under an agreement with Dr. Michał Daszkiewicz, a professor at the University of Gdańsk’s Faculty of Social Sciences and editor-in-chief of Educational Role of Language (ERL). Within this framework, Dr. Mahnaz Norouzi, Associate Professor of Russian, has been nominated for peer-review cooperation with CRE, while Dr. Mehdi Mohajer, Assistant Professor of French, and Dr. Shima Ebrahimi, Associate Professor of Linguistics, have been nominated to cooperate with ERL. These agreements aim to facilitate the exchange of peer-review expertise, strengthen scholarly evaluation and build closer links between academic journals.
