Journal profile

About the Journal

Mission, scope and publication principles of Russian Language Studies.

Official identity

Russian Language Studies is the English title of دوفصلنامه مطالعات زبان روسی. The journal is published under the ownership of Mohammadmahdi Rubin and is currently in the establishment phase.

Ministry of Culture and Islamic Guidance license: 94254 · License date: 1402/05/02 · Frequency: two issues per year.

Mission

The journal is being developed as a scholarly platform for original research on Russian language and its educational, literary, cultural and translation-related dimensions.

Scope

Russian Language Studies welcomes research in Russian linguistics, Russian as a foreign language, language education, applied linguistics, discourse and pragmatics, sociolinguistics, translation and interpreting, Russian literature, cultural studies, intercultural communication, comparative studies and related interdisciplinary fields.

Publication status

Peer review

The journal has defined an establishment-phase review framework. The final reviewer arrangements will be confirmed before formal publication operations.

Publication ethics

Policies address originality, plagiarism, authorship, conflicts of interest, confidentiality, corrections and retractions.

Open access and licensing

Access and licensing terms will be formally announced before regular publication begins.

Scholarly metadata

ISSN, DOI, indexing and preservation information will be added only after official registration or confirmation.

Languages

The website is available in Persian, English and Russian. Publication languages will be governed by the final editorial policy.
