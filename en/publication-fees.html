Publication Fees

Transparent information about charges associated with manuscripts.

Current status

During the journal's launch phase, the publication fee policy has not yet been finalized. Any submission, review, editing, or publication charge, if introduced, will be announced clearly on this page before manuscripts are processed.

Transparency

Editorial decisions must not be based on an author's ability to pay. Any applicable charges should be defined separately from peer review and communicated clearly.
