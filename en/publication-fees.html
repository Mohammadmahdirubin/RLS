Publication Fees

Transparent information about charges associated with manuscripts.

Article Processing and DOI Fee

The journal does not charge any fees for manuscript submission, peer review, or editing. However, to cover the costs of processing, purchasing, and assigning a Digital Object Identifier (DOI), a fee of 750,000 tomans is charged for each accepted article, only after its final acceptance. This is the current fee.

Transparency and Editorial Independence

No fee is charged at the submission stage or during peer review. The fee for DOI processing, purchase, and assignment is payable only after the article has been finally accepted. Payment has no influence on scholarly evaluation, the peer-review process, or the final editorial decision. Any change to the fee or charging policy will be announced clearly on this page before it takes effect.
