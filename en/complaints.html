Complaints and Appeals

How to raise and review complaints and appeals concerning editorial processes.

Submitting a complaint

Complaints, appeals or reports concerning editorial processes may be sent to mm.rubin@ut.ac.ir with a clear description, manuscript information where applicable and supporting evidence.

Appeals

An appeal should identify a review-process error or a specific, evidence-based issue in the scientific assessment. Disagreement alone does not require a decision to be changed.

Editorial and ethical complaints

Concerns about conflicts of interest, confidentiality, inappropriate conduct, research misconduct or procedural errors will be reviewed and, where appropriate, assigned to another qualified person.

Confidentiality and independence

Complaint information will be kept confidential as far as practicable. A person whose own decision or interest is directly involved should not be the sole decision-maker for that complaint.
