3. Indicators that can be activated after publication begins

  • Number of submissions by year and issue
  • Number of accepted, rejected and under-review articles
  • Annual acceptance rate
  • Average initial screening and review times
  • Number of articles published in each issue
  • Article view and download statistics after the archive is created
  • Geographic distribution of authors once sufficient data exist

4. Transparency principle

No figure, acceptance rate, review time, number of reviewers, number of authors or citation indicator will be published on the website without statistical support.
