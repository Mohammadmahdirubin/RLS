EDITORIAL GOVERNANCE

Editorial Policies & Ethics

Core standards for editorial decisions, peer review and research integrity.

Policy status: This page is a framework for the journal's establishment phase. The final policy text should be formally approved before manuscripts are accepted.

Editorial decision-making

Editors should evaluate submissions on scholarly quality, relevance to the journal's scope, originality and methodological soundness. Editorial decisions should not be based on personal characteristics, institutional affiliation or other irrelevant considerations.

Peer review

The intended model is external peer review with confidentiality maintained during the review process. Reviewers should assess the manuscript fairly, identify substantive concerns and disclose any conflict of interest that could affect their judgment.

Research integrity

Originality & plagiarism

Manuscripts must be original. Suspected plagiarism, duplicate publication or substantial unattributed overlap will be investigated according to the journal's adopted procedure.

Conflicts of interest

Authors, editors and reviewers should disclose relationships or interests that could influence the evaluation or publication of a manuscript.

Corrections

Material errors discovered after publication should be corrected transparently with an appropriate notice linked to the article.

Retractions

Retraction may be considered when published findings are demonstrably unreliable or publication integrity has been seriously compromised.

Authorship

All listed authors should have made a meaningful contribution and approved the submitted version. Changes to authorship after submission should require documented agreement from the relevant authors.

Data, images and permissions

Authors are responsible for obtaining permission to publish third-party material where required and for reporting research methods and evidence accurately.
