PRIVACY

Privacy Statement

How information supplied through the journal website is intended to be handled.

This preliminary website does not operate a manuscript-management database. Any future submission form or journal platform should publish a complete privacy statement before collecting author or reviewer information.

Planned principles

  • Personal information will be collected only for legitimate editorial and publication purposes.
  • Author and reviewer information will be handled confidentially and will not be publicly displayed unless required for publication or expressly authorized.
  • Peer-review correspondence and confidential review material should not be published in the public GitHub repository.
  • Third-party services used for forms, email or analytics should be identified in the final privacy notice.

Current status

This page is a preliminary policy framework and is not a substitute for a legally reviewed privacy notice.
