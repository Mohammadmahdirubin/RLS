JOURNAL PROFILE

About the Journal

Mission, scope and publication principles of Russian Language & Linguistics Studies (RLLS).

Mission

Russian Language & Linguistics Studies (RLLS) is being developed as a scholarly venue for original research on the Russian language and its educational, literary, cultural and translational contexts. The journal aims to provide a clear and professionally organized platform for researchers, university teachers, translators and advanced scholars.

Scope

The journal welcomes rigorous research in Russian linguistics, applied linguistics, Russian as a Foreign Language, language education, discourse and pragmatics, sociolinguistics, translation and interpreting, Russian literature, cultural studies, intercultural communication and related interdisciplinary areas.

Publication model

Open Access

The intended model is free access to published research for readers. The final licensing and copyright policy will be stated before publication begins.

Peer Review

Appropriate manuscripts will undergo editorial screening followed by external peer review according to the journal's formally adopted policy.

Publication Ethics

The journal will maintain policies covering originality, plagiarism, conflicts of interest, corrections, retractions and reviewer confidentiality.

Metadata

ISSN, DOI, indexing and preservation information will be added only after the relevant registrations or agreements are completed.

Languages

The website is designed for multilingual development. English, Persian and Russian interfaces/content may be introduced progressively according to the journal's editorial policy.
