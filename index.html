About the Journal

Russian Language & Linguistics Studies (RLLS) is an academic journal dedicated to original research and scholarly discussion concerning the Russian language and its teaching, linguistic structure, literature, culture, translation, and related interdisciplinary fields.

Open Access

Published content is intended to be freely accessible to readers.

Peer Review

Submissions are evaluated through an editorial and peer-review process before publication.

International Scope

The journal welcomes relevant research from scholars working in different countries and academic traditions.

02

Aims & Scope

  • Russian linguistics and applied linguistics
  • Russian as a Foreign Language (RFL)
  • Language teaching and teacher education
  • Translation and interpreting studies
  • Russian literature and literary studies
  • Russian culture and intercultural communication
  • Discourse, pragmatics and sociolinguistics
  • Comparative and interdisciplinary studies
The journal seeks research that connects rigorous linguistic or literary analysis with meaningful questions in language, culture, education, translation, and society.

04

Author Guidelines

1. Originality

Manuscripts must be original and not under simultaneous consideration elsewhere.

2. File format

Submit the manuscript as Microsoft Word (.docx) unless otherwise specified.

3. Structure

Include title, author information, abstract, keywords, main text, references, and any required declarations.

4. Review

Suitable submissions may be sent for external peer review after editorial screening.

5. Ethics

Authors are responsible for originality, accurate attribution, and disclosure of conflicts of interest.

6. Fees

No publication fee is claimed on this preliminary site. This policy must be formally approved before publication.
